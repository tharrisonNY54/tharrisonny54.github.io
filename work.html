W.01

NASA Aspera Mission

Software Developer May 2026 — present University of Arizona

Aspera is a NASA SmallSat built to observe extreme-ultraviolet emission from the hot gas around galaxies, the circumgalactic medium, which has not been mapped at these wavelengths before.

I write code that analyzes the science data Aspera will send down after launch, and I fix bugs in it as they come up. A lot of the work is making sure what we build stays inside the constraints NASA sets for the mission. I have also worked on the mission’s SPICE kernels, which describe where the spacecraft is and where it is pointing.

Most of the specifics are covered by export control, so this is about as much detail as I can give here.

Python Data analysis SPICE EUV astronomy
W.02

Center for Digital Humanities

Student Developer May 2025 — present University of Arizona

Lidar surveys of Jewish cemeteries in Curaçao, 3D scans of museum artifacts, volumetric recordings of community members, and the web pipelines that make them public.

I build and maintain the center’s Three.js viewers and the AWS S3 and CloudFront pipeline behind them. The scan on the home page came off our stage and runs on the same stack.

When Niantic shut down 8th Wall, the center’s AR projects were going to stop working. Niantic open sourced part of their code and left an export path, so I used it to build distributions and rehosted more than 200 of them on our CDN, then sent clients the new links so their services were not interrupted. It took months and I am still finishing it. Some of what would have been lost is Holocaust survivor recordings and university lectures.

I have also taught volumetric capture and AR workflows to graduate courses. The Smithsonian is coming out in a few months to record with us for a traveling exhibit.

Three.js Volumetric capture LIDAR Gaussian splatting AWS S3 / CloudFront Photogrammetry
