Help

This website is how our group signs up to play. On a computer the main page is a table: the days of play run across the top, and the members run down the left side. Your row shows what you have signed up for; a column shows who is playing on that day. On a phone the same sheet is shown one day at a time instead.

On a phone

The table needs a wide screen, so a phone opens the sheet one day at a time instead. You get a card for each day of play, with your own answer at the top and a line underneath you can tap to see who else is in. The two buttons at the top of the page, Day by day and Full grid, switch between the cards and the table. Whichever you pick is remembered on that device.

Both work the same way: make your choices, then press Submit Changes at the bottom of the page. Nothing is saved until you do.

The colors in the table

  • Blank — you have not said anything yet.
  • N Not available — you cannot play that day.
  • A Available — you signed up, but there are not yet enough members to make a game.
  • P Playing — you are in a game that day.

How to sign up

In the table, the last row of the page is where you make changes. It has one small box for each day. Click the arrow on a day's box and choose one of three things. On a phone the same three choices are buttons on each day's card:

  • A — I am available and I want to play.
  • N — I cannot play that day.
  • O — I have not decided yet.

Change as many days as you like, then click Submit Changes. If you change your mind before submitting, click Reset and your changes are undone.

How the draw works

A game needs four players. When you submit, the site recalculates the whole sheet. Everyone who is available is put in order — regular members first, then substitutes, and within each group, whoever signed up earliest comes first.

Players are then placed into complete games of four. If five people are available, four turn green and play, and the fifth stays yellow and waits. That is why your A can turn into a P without you doing anything — somebody else signed up and filled the game. If somebody drops out, the person who signed up last loses the spot and goes back to yellow.

The number next to the day at the top of each column is how many games are set for that day. If your status changes to Playing, the site will email you.

The names and the numbers

  • Black names are regular members.
  • Blue names are substitutes.
  • Red names are website administrators.

The numbered button to the left of each name is that member's row number. The button shown in bright blue marks the last person who signed up for the next day of play, so you can see at a glance whether anything has moved since you last looked.

The two Guest rows at the bottom are for signing up someone from outside the group when there are not enough members to fill a game.

Contact Information

The Contact Information button opens a page listing every member with their phone numbers and email address. Click an address to write to that person, or use the buttons at the bottom to email the whole group, just the people playing, just the people waiting, or only the members you tick.

Signing in and out

You sign in with the Sign Up Sheet ID and your own User Password. Tick Remember this login and this computer will fill them in for you for a week.

You stay signed in until you close your browser. If you share a computer, click Logout when you are finished so nobody else can change your sign ups.

Your password is your phone number. If you cannot get in, ask one of the administrators (the red names) and they will send your sign in details to you again.

For administrators

Administrators see an extra Website Maint button. It changes the red announcement at the top of the sheet, adds, edits and removes members, sends a member their welcome message, and adjusts the settings — how many days show, how many players make a game, how many games can run at once, and how guests are treated in the draw.

Administrators can also edit anybody's row: click the numbered button next to a member's name and the bottom row switches to that member. The Signing up box at the top of the page does the same thing and works in both layouts.
