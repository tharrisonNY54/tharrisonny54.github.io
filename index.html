About

I study computer science at the University of Arizona, with minors in mathematics and AI, on an accelerated track toward a master’s in 2029.

My work sits where geometry meets hardware. On the Aspera mission I write SPICE tooling that turns orbital data into pointing and visibility answers. At the Center for Digital Humanities I scan people and places — LIDAR in Curaçao cemeteries, volumetric capture stages — and build the Three.js pipelines that put them on the web.

When Niantic shut down 8th Wall, I reverse engineered its volumetric format and moved 200+ AR projects onto university infrastructure. I like problems shaped like that one. The figure on the left is one of our captures — me, as 88,704 points.
