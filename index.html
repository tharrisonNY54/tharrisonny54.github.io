About

I study computer science at the University of Arizona, with minors in mathematics and AI. I finish my bachelor’s in December 2027 and my master’s in May 2029.

On the Aspera mission I write code that analyzes the science data the spacecraft will send down after launch. At the Center for Digital Humanities I scan people and places, including lidar mapping of Jewish cemeteries in Curaçao and volumetric recordings of community members, and I build the Three.js pipelines that put them on the web.

When Niantic shut down 8th Wall, our lab’s AR projects were going to stop working. I spent months exporting them and rehosting more than 200 on our CDN so the links kept working. Some of them are Holocaust survivor recordings and university lectures that would have been unusable otherwise. The figure on the left is one of our captures. It’s me, as 88,704 points.
